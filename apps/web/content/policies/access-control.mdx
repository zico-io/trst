# Access Control Policy

**Last reviewed:** January 15, 2026

## Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for controlling access to Bask Health systems, applications, and data to ensure that only authorized individuals can access resources appropriate to their role.

## Scope

This policy applies to all Bask Health employees, contractors, vendors, and any third parties who have been granted access to Bask Health systems or data.

## Principles

Access control at Bask Health follows the principle of **least privilege** — users are granted only the permissions they need to perform their job function, and no more.

## Requirements

### User Access Management

- All user accounts must be provisioned through the HR onboarding process
- Access requests require manager approval and are logged in our access request system
- Privileged access (admin, production systems) requires additional approval from the Security team
- Access is reviewed quarterly; unused accounts are disabled after 90 days of inactivity

### Authentication

- All systems must require multi-factor authentication (MFA) for interactive login
- Passwords must meet a minimum length of 16 characters
- Single Sign-On (SSO) is the preferred authentication method for all SaaS tools

### Remote Access

- VPN or zero-trust network access is required for all remote connections to internal systems
- Remote desktop sessions must be logged and are subject to review

### Termination

- Access to all systems is revoked within 4 hours of employment termination
- Privileged access is revoked immediately upon notification

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

*This policy is reviewed annually or following any material change to our environment.*
