# Incident Response Policy

**Last reviewed:** March 10, 2026

## Purpose

This policy establishes a structured approach for detecting, responding to, and recovering from security incidents to minimize impact on Bask Health operations and patient data.

## Scope

This policy applies to all security incidents affecting Bask Health systems, including but not limited to: data breaches, ransomware, unauthorized access, system outages caused by malicious activity, and PHI exposure.

## Incident Classification

| Severity | Description | Response Time |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **P1 – Critical** | Active breach, PHI exposure, ransomware | Immediate (24/7) |
| **P2 – High** | Suspected breach, significant system compromise | Within 1 hour |
| **P3 – Medium** | Anomalous behavior, policy violation | Within 4 hours |
| **P4 – Low** | Minor policy violation, unsuccessful attack | Within 24 hours |

## Response Phases

### 1. Detection & Triage

- Security alerts are monitored 24/7 via our SIEM platform
- Any team member who suspects a security incident must report it immediately to security@bask.health or via Slack #security-incidents
- The on-call security engineer triages and classifies the incident within 30 minutes

### 2. Containment

- Affected systems are isolated to prevent lateral movement
- Credentials suspected of compromise are immediately rotated
- Network segmentation is applied as needed

### 3. Investigation & Eradication

- Root cause analysis is performed to identify the attack vector
- All affected systems are forensically imaged before remediation
- Malware, backdoors, and unauthorized access paths are removed

### 4. Recovery

- Systems are restored from verified clean backups or rebuilt from infrastructure-as-code
- Enhanced monitoring is applied to restored systems for 30 days post-incident

### 5. Notification

**HIPAA Breach Notification:**
- Individuals affected by a PHI breach are notified within 60 days of discovery
- HHS is notified per HIPAA Breach Notification Rule timelines
- If more than 500 individuals are affected, prominent media outlets in the affected state are also notified

**Customer Notification:**
- Enterprise customers are notified within 72 hours of a confirmed breach affecting their data

### 6. Post-Incident Review

- A blameless post-mortem is conducted within 5 business days
- Findings and corrective actions are documented and tracked to completion
