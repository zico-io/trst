# Data Retention Policy

**Last reviewed:** February 1, 2026

## Purpose

This policy defines how long Bask Health retains different categories of data and establishes procedures for secure disposal when data is no longer needed.

## Scope

This policy covers all data collected, processed, or stored by Bask Health systems, including patient health information, business records, logs, and backups.

## Retention Schedules

### Protected Health Information (PHI)

Consistent with HIPAA requirements and applicable state laws:

- **Medical records:** 7 years from the date of the last service (10 years for minors)
- **Billing records:** 7 years
- **Authorization forms:** 6 years from date of creation or last effective date

### Business and Operational Data

- **Financial records:** 7 years
- **HR records:** Duration of employment plus 7 years
- **Contracts and agreements:** Duration of contract plus 7 years
- **Security incident records:** 5 years

### System and Application Logs

- **Access logs:** 1 year
- **Security event logs:** 2 years
- **Application error logs:** 90 days
- **Audit trails:** 7 years (HIPAA requirement)

### Backup Retention

- **Daily backups:** Retained for 30 days
- **Weekly backups:** Retained for 90 days
- **Monthly backups:** Retained for 1 year
- **Annual backups:** Retained for 7 years

## Data Disposal

When data reaches the end of its retention period:

1. **Electronic data** is securely overwritten using NIST 800-88 compliant methods, or storage media is physically destroyed
2. **Paper records** containing PHI are cross-cut shredded using a certified destruction vendor
3. **Disposal is logged** with date, method, and type of data destroyed

## Legal Holds

Data subject to litigation holds or regulatory investigations is exempt from normal retention schedules and must not be destroyed until the hold is released by Legal.
